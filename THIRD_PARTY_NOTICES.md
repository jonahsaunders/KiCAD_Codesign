# Third-party notices

The practice PCB and schematic embed library elements from the KiCad community libraries shipped with KiCad 10.0.6:

- `Device:R` and `Device:C` schematic symbols.
- `Resistor_SMD:R_0805_2012Metric` and `Capacitor_SMD:C_0805_2012Metric` footprints.

Copyright belongs to the respective KiCad library contributors. The elements are placed in standalone practice designs with project-specific references, values, positions, and explanatory text. They are not distributed here as a separate library collection.
